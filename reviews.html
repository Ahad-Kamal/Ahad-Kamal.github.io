Octopath Traveler 2

13 Sentinels: Aegis Rim

Normally confusion isn't something you want in your game, but never before id I find so much enjoyment from being so confused than when I played through this game. 13 Sentinels is all about piecing together the game's narrative which is already complex enough as is but then you add in the fact that the story is split across 13 different characters and that the story is not told in chronological order and you have one hell of a crazy convoluted story.
Now while it may sound like a daunting challenge, piecing together the plot is actually incredibly exciting and once I was hooked I could not put this game down. The game combines every single sci-fi trope in existance yet somehow manages to not be derivative whatsoever. The characters are all great, though some feel a bit more fleshed out than others and while the combat seems quite dull at first with it being a wierd rts/tower defense hybrid, it actually become quite fun once you get the hang of things.
I truly think that this game is a masterclass in storytelling and a modern mastepiece which is why it's such a shame that not many people have played it and if you have even a slight interest in visual novels then I highly reccomend this game.
